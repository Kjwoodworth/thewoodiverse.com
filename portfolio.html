02

Event Strategy, Coordination & Communications

Agility: Hype To Hope To Health

CMI Media Group's Annual Flagship Event · Philadelphia, PA · September 8, 2025

Event Strategy Executive Coordination Speaker Relations & NDAs PR Execution Social Media Coverage Brand Communications

Throughout Q3 2025, I planned, promoted, coordinated, and helped curate CMI Media Group's largest event of the year — Agility: Hype To Hope To Health — a full-scale healthcare marketing conference held at the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts in Philadelphia.

The event's theme was deeply intentional. Following a period of uncertainty brought on by the pandemic and the shift to remote work, Agility 2025 was designed not just as a conference, but as a moment of reconnection — bringing those in the industry together in person, providing a sense of relief and renewed energy after years of distance. It was about seeing faces, building relationships, and rediscovering the power of being in the same room.

Centered around the future outlook of healthcare and the genuine hope that innovation holds for the industry, the event gave clients, partners, and attendees unique perspectives on the critical topics and trends shaping healthcare marketing — and challenged them to think boldly about what comes next.

My responsibilities spanned the full event lifecycle: pre-event social media promotion, managing executive speaker coordination, logistics, and compliance documentation for nationally recognized industry leaders including Mick Ebeling (Not Impossible Labs) and Daymond John (Shark Tank / FUBU), managing on-site admission and operations, and producing live and post-event social coverage including highlights and recap content.

Outcome & Impact

Agility 2025 was recognized as one of the industry's greatest successes — earning a Gold MarCom Award for Excellence in Strategic Communications under the "Special Event" category, an award I helped research, draft, and submit from start to finish. The event was celebrated for the uniqueness of its experience and the value it delivered to attendees — bringing together clients, partners, and industry leaders at a moment when in-person connection meant more than ever.
